# Woodworking Lathe

> **Roster:** [_Prop_List](../_Prop_List.md) · **Technical:** [Resource Processing (tech)](../../../../Technical_Design/Shop/Resource_Processing.md)

## Description & Role

Arcane lumber is loaded; the player holds the chisel (continuous) while the wood rotates, then chooses Hilt, Short Shaft or Long Shaft.

## Behaviour & States

- **Input → output:** Arcane timber → rough foundation
- **Unlocked at:** Tier 2

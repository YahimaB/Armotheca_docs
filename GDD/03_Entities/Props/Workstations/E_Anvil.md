# Anvil

> **Roster:** [_Prop_List](../_Prop_List.md) · **Technical:** [Resource Processing (tech)](../../../../Technical_Design/Shop/Resource_Processing.md)

## Description & Role

Shapes the hot ingot into a blade with the anvil-strike mini-game. Exclusive: one player at a time.

## Behaviour & States

- **Input → output:** Hot ingot → unquenched blade
- **Unlocked at:** Tier 1 (Basic Anvil)

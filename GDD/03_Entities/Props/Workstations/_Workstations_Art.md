# Workstations — Art

> **Roster:** [_Prop_List](../_Prop_List.md) · **Guidelines:** [3D Modeling Guidelines](../../../../Guidelines/Modeling_Guidelines.md)

## Modeling Requirements

- Workstations are hero objects: the highest detail in the workshop, with a unique silhouette per station so players tell them apart across the room.
- Sockets and interaction points are named empties (`Socket_Input`, `Socket_Output`, `Interact_Point`) for the interaction system.
- Moving parts (bellows, lathe chuck, mallet, valves) are separate objects named `Part_<Name>`, each with its own pivot, so they can be animated.

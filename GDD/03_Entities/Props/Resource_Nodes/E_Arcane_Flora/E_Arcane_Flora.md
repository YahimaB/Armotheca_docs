# Arcane Flora

> **Roster:** [_Prop_List](../../_Prop_List.md) · **Technical:** [Resource_Nodes](../../../../../Technical_Design/Exploration/Resource_Nodes.md)

## Description & Role

Arcane trees. Harvested with an axe. Each node type has a distinct silhouette and colour, readable from across the realm. Rare variants glow.

## Behaviour & States

Intact → struck (visible damage per hit) → broken (drops yield, does not regrow this expedition). See [M_Harvesting](../../../../02_Systems/Exploration/M_Harvesting.md).

## Stats

Hits per node: [Exploration › Nodes](../../../../06_Balancing/Exploration.md#nodes).

## Outputs

Primary yield: Heartwood, Ancient Bark. Used for: Hilts, wand and staff shafts.

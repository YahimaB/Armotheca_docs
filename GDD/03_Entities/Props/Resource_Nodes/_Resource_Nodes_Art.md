# Resource Nodes — Art

> **Roster:** [_Prop_List](../_Prop_List.md) · **Guidelines:** [3D Modeling Guidelines](../../../../Guidelines/Modeling_Guidelines.md)

## Modeling Requirements

- Model one mesh per state in the node's Behaviour & States; all states share one pivot. Damage between two states may be shown by VFX instead of a mesh of its own.
- Each node type reads by colour from far away: a distinct hue per ore, wood or crystal, consistent with its [element colour](../../../../Guidelines/Art_Direction/Visual_Style.md#1-element-colours).

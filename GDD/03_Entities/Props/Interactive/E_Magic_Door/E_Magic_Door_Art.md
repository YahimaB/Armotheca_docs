# Magic Door — Art

> **Entity:** [E_Magic_Door](E_Magic_Door.md)

## Modeling Requirements

- Model the frame only; the portal surface, swirl and rune animation are VFX and shader work. Reference: AF_4 in [Modeling Guidelines §2](../../../../../Guidelines/Modeling_Guidelines.md#per-image-notes).
- The runes get an emission mask so the door can light up per state in [E_Magic_Door › Behaviour & States](E_Magic_Door.md#behaviour--states).
- Leave space on the frame for a price plaque and for the names of expedition members (both world-space UI).

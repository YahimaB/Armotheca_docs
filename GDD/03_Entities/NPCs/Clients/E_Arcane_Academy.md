# Arcane Academy (The Perfectionists)

> **Roster:** [_Client_Roster](_Client_Roster.md) · **Technical:** [_Orders_Module › Data](../../../../Technical_Design/Orders/_Orders_Module.md#data)

## Description & Role

Elitist scholars and archmages researching planar anomalies. Relaxed timing but uncompromising standards — the counterpoint to frantic SOS orders.

## Behaviour & States

- **Demand pattern:** staves and wands only; strict demand for Masterwork or Flawless and specific arcane cores.
- **Tolerance:** **zero tolerance for Crude components.** Delivering anything below the requested tier results in immediate contract rejection and a severe reputation penalty.
- **Available from:** Tier 2 ([M_Workshop_Tiers](../../../02_Systems/Progression/M_Workshop_Tiers.md)).

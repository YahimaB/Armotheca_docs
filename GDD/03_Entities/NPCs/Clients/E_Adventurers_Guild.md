# Adventurers Guild (The Bread & Butter)

> **Roster:** [_Client_Roster](_Client_Roster.md) · **Technical:** [Order System (tech)](../../../../Technical_Design/Shop/Order_System.md)

## Description & Role

Pragmatic monster hunters needing dependable gear. The steady, high-volume client that keeps cash flowing.

## Behaviour & States

- **Demand pattern:** 1 Sword or 1 Wand; accepts Standard or Masterwork; general elements.
- **Timing:** relaxed.
- **Available from:** Tier 1 ([M_Workshop_Tiers](../../../02_Systems/Progression/M_Workshop_Tiers.md)).

## Stats

Lifespan, rewards and penalty: [Orders › Client Archetypes](../../../06_Balancing/Orders.md#client-archetypes).

## Outputs

Steady gold and Guild reputation. Masterwork deliveries are eligible for Guild bonuses ([M_Quality](../../../02_Systems/Crafting/M_Quality.md)). Failure costs negligible reputation.

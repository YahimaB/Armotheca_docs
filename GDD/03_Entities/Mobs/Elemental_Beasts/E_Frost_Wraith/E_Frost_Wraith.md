> **Roster:** [_Mob_Roster](../../_Mob_Roster.md) · **Technical:** [Mob_AI](../../../../../Technical_Design/Exploration/Mob_AI.md)

## Description & Role

Elemental Beast of [L04_Frostbite_Cavern](../../../../04_Locations/L04_Frostbite_Cavern/L04_Frostbite_Cavern.md), found mainly in the Deep Grounds. Not staggered by sword hits. Shared mob rules: [_Mob_Roster](../../_Mob_Roster.md).

## Behaviour & States

- **Chaser:** Closes distance and attacks with a telegraphed lunge or bite. Counter: roll through the lunge, then swing.

# The Uber-Weapon (Endgame Ritual)

> **Zone:** Shop · **Technical counterpart:** [TechnicalDesign/Shop/UberWeapon](../../TechnicalDesign/Shop/UberWeapon.md) · **Prerequisite:** [Shop Progression](ShopProgression.md) Tier 3

## Design Intent

The Uber-Weapon — **The Primordial Armament** — is the legendary magnum opus of the Armotheca lineage and the campaign's win condition. It gives every commission and expedition a long-term purpose, and its forging is not a silent menu click but an intense, synchronized co-op event that uses every station the team has mastered.

---

## Player Experience Goals

- **Climactic Climax:** The final forge is a set-piece where every player has a role under pressure.
- **Long-Term Anticipation:** Each catalyst brought home is a visible, celebrated step toward the end.
- **Mastery Showcase:** The ritual reuses the workshop's manual stations at a higher intensity, rewarding skills built over the campaign.

---

## References

- **Factorio (Rocket Launch):** A clear macro-objective that ends the campaign.
- **Subnautica (Neptune Rocket):** A multi-phase construction project requiring rare ingredients from the most dangerous places in the world.
- **Overcooked (final level):** A frantic finale combining every mechanic learned.

## The 4-Phase Forging Ritual

Requires Tier 3 (The Great Crucible) and all 4 catalysts. Players start the ritual from the Crucible (instant interaction).

1. **Phase 1 — Harmonic Smelting (Furnace Team):** The Ignis Core and Astral Prism are melted in the Great Crucible. Temperature must be held in range through synchronized bellows pumping (cooperative continuous interaction).
2. **Phase 2 — Arcane Turning (Carpentry Team):** The Sylvan Spire is carved into an Adamantine Shaft on the high-speed lathe.
3. **Phase 3 — The Glacial Quench (Dual Action):** The molten celestial blade is plunged into the Glacial Heart solution at the exact moment the temperature needle aligns.
4. **Phase 4 — Final Binding:** Both components are locked into the Grand Assembly Bench, and all active players strike their mallets simultaneously to seal the weapon.

- The ritual scales to the number of players present; with fewer than 4, phases run sequentially instead of in parallel.
- Failing a phase sets the ritual back to the start of that phase, never destroys catalysts.

---

## Endgame Payoff

On completion the campaign victory screen plays, celebrating team stats, and **Prestige Endless Mode** unlocks: chaos modifiers, unstable multi-door breaches, celestial clients and global high-score ladders.
